How I work

I work across the whole product cycle: understand people, define the problem, prototype the solution and keep the team moving.

Research

I lead qualitative studies that start from lived experience, then analyze what people share into themes a team can act on.

  • Sister circles
  • Interviews
  • Surveys
  • Digital Journals
  • Thematic analysis

Design

I facilitate participatory and speculative design workshops where the people affected define the problem and sketch the future.

  • Participatory design
  • Speculative design
  • User stories
  • Workshop facilitation

Prototype

I prototype with fabrication tools and wearable electronics, so ideas become something people can hold and test.

  • LilyPad Arduino
  • E-textiles
  • 3D printing
  • Cricut
  • Embroidery

Lead

I plan studies end to end, coordinate research teams and keep stakeholders aligned, from protocol to final presentation.

  • Project planning
  • Team coordination
  • Stakeholder workshops
  • Research ethics and IRB

About me

I've been a tech nerd since my first Nintendo. Now I build spaces where more Black women get to be one too.

My research sits where human-computer interaction, physical computing and health equity meet.

I design hands-on experiences, from makerspaces to wearable prototypes, that position Black women as inventors and producers of technology rather than its afterthought. I ground that work in Black feminist thought and participatory methods, so the people I design with shape both the questions and the answers.

Outside the lab, I do tech consulting and advocate for wellness and for women in computing.
